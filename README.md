### 📂 Featured Projects

#### 🦞 [Distribution Analysis Pipeline: Panulirus spp.](https://github.com/matheus9data/matheus9dados)

- **Stack:** `R` `ggplot2` `terra` `sf`
- **Problem:** Unstructured biological data requiring standardization for accurate geographic mapping.
- **Solution:** R pipeline that ingests raw data, performs coordinate cleaning and standardization, and generates automated spatial distribution maps.

---

#### 🌍 [Statistical Ecological Niche Modeling](https://github.com/matheus9data/matheus9dados)

- **Stack:** `R` `Quantitative Statistics`
- **Problem:** Need to predict species occurrence based on cross-referenced environmental and climatic variables.
- **Solution:** Script focused on ingestion and processing of complex multi-variable data to generate robust predictive models.

---

#### 🐟 [ICCAT — South Atlantic Tuna Bycatch Analysis](https://github.com/matheus9data/matheus9dados)

- **Stack:** `R` `Python` `Pandas`
- **Problem:** Historical database with 89,000+ rows and 53 columns covering 40 years of tuna bycatch records in the South Atlantic — with inconsistencies, missing values, and heterogeneous structure.
- **Solution:** Large-scale data cleaning, standardization, and quality control pipeline, with temporal and geographic consistency validation for population dynamics analysis.
